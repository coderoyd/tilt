What stays on your iPhone

TILT keeps the following only on your device, so the app can work:

  • Your sessions. How long each focus and break session ran, whether it finished, pauses, and the label you chose (for example Study). This powers the calendar and Insights.
  • Your settings. Palette and colours, timer font, soundscape, presets, labels and similar preferences.
  • Photos you choose. If you pick photos as a timer background, TILT saves copies in its own storage on your iPhone. It can only see the photos you pick, never the rest of your library, and never uploads them.

Like other app data, this may be included in your iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles according to your settings.

Purchases

TILT Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple gives us anonymised sales reports, such as the number of subscriptions in each country.

What Apple may share with us

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us aggregated, anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. We can't identify you from them. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us

If you send feedback to code@roydsantiago.com, we receive your email address and message. We use them only to reply, never add you to a mailing list, and will delete them if you ask.

Links out of the app

Rate, Share, Terms and Manage Subscription open Apple's App Store, share sheet or web pages, which are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

TILT is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete TILT from your iPhone and everything it stored goes with it: sessions, settings and photo copies. Subscriptions are managed in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. For meaningful changes, we'll also mention them in the app's release notes.

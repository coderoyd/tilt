What stays on your iPhone

TILT keeps these on your device, and only there, so the app can work:

  • Your sessions. How long each FOCUS and BREAK ran, whether it finished, pauses, and the label you picked (like Study). This powers the calendar and Insights.
  • Your settings. Palette and colors, timer font, soundscape, presets and labels.
  • Photos you pick. If you choose photos as a timer background, TILT keeps copies in its own storage on your iPhone. It only sees the photos you pick, never the rest of your library, and never uploads them.

Like any app data, this can be part of your iPhone or iCloud backup, which Apple handles based on your settings.

Purchases

TILT Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment. We never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple sends us anonymized sales reports, like how many subscriptions there are in each country.

What Apple may share

If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage stats. We can't identify you from them. Turn this off anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you email us

Send feedback to code@roydsantiago.com and we get your email address and message. We use them only to reply, never add you to a mailing list, and delete them if you ask.

Links out of the app

Rate, Share, Terms and Manage Subscription open Apple's App Store, share sheet or web pages, covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

TILT is for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Deleting your data

Delete TILT and everything it stored goes with it: sessions, settings and photo copies. Manage subscriptions in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Bigger changes also go in the release notes.
